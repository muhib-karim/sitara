# Sitara

An independent GPL-3.0 Android music player for listeners who want fast access to their queue, artwork and lyrics. Kotlin/Compose UI, Media3 playback, fake catalog during foundation development. The original star-and-orbit identity is defined in docs/design/DESIGN_SPEC.md.

Core jobs: browse music, play and manage a queue, seek, follow lyrics and adjust playback settings. Native touch targets, TalkBack actions, reduced motion and readable light/dark surfaces are product requirements. No copied branding, proprietary artwork or inert product controls.
