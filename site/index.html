Sitara

Your music, beautifully.

A free YouTube Music client for Android where every transition rides a spring, the chrome is glass, colour comes from the cover and the lyrics sing along word by word.

Made to feel finished

The details you notice every time you press play.

  • Spring motion

    The player sheet follows your finger and hands its release velocity to a critically damped spring. No snaps, no overshoot.

  • Colour from the cover

    Backdrop and accents are tinted from the artwork, with contrast checked in code. Or choose Material You, or pure AMOLED black.

  • Karaoke lyrics

    Word-level fill from enhanced LRC timing, a focused active line and tap-to-seek. Lyrics share the pager with Up next.

  • Glass you can read

    A floating pill tab bar and frosted sheets with real blur on Android 12+, and a tuned tinted scrim on older devices.

  • Gestures that make sense

    Swipe the mini player up to open, down to hide, sideways to skip. Shuffle restores your order when you turn it off.

  • Sound that stays level

    Loudness matched to −14 LUFS, smooth crossfade curves and a sleep timer that can wait for the end of the song.

Why Sitara

Existing clients cover the basics well. Sitara is built for what comes after: reliability you can see, data you own and an app that is a pleasure to hold.

Reliable by design
The stream source sits behind an interface with health checks and automatic fallback, with a health dashboard planned so you can see what is working.
Your data, your storage
Sitara Sync is planned to keep library, likes and settings in step across devices, end-to-end encrypted, through storage you already own.
Lyrics for everyone
Transliteration for Bangla, Hindi and Urdu is on the roadmap, so you can sing along in any script.
Accessible from day one
TalkBack labels, 48 dp targets, 200% font scale and a reduced-motion mode are built into the design system.
Built in the open
GPL-3.0, a public test suite, screenshot tests and a reproducible build pipeline. No account required.

Roadmap

Shipped in phases, each one tested and usable.

  1. Phase A · now

    Foundation

    Design system, glass tab bar, mini and full player on springs, queue, karaoke lyrics engine, sleep timer, theme modes, green CI and screenshot tests.

  2. Phase B

    Real source

    Search and browse, stream resolution with health checks and fallback, downloads, local files, synced lyrics, playlists, import and backup.

  3. Phase C

    Accounts and connection

    Library sync, scrobbling, widgets, Android Auto, Cast, Sitara Sync and LAN remote control.

  4. Phase D

    Premium touches

    Animated canvas, automix, lyric translation and transliteration, Wear OS remote and a TV layout.
